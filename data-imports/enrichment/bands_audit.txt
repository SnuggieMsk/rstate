**Systematic finding:** the celebrated "prime Chennai land trades above built-up" inversion is, in six of its seven instances (Adyar, Nungambakkam, Anna Nagar, Mylapore, Choolaimedu, Keelkattalai, Adambakkam), **an artifact of the 99acres land series with not one recomputed transaction behind it.** The inversions that *are* evidenced — Neelankarai (7 comps), Palavakkam (4 recomputed), Thiruvottiyur (18), Korattur (6), Besant Nagar (4, two circular) — are the ones to keep. Do not let the verified cases launder the unverified ones.

### TIER 3 — The band is an outlier-drop away from saying the opposite.

- **Ambattur** (plot 8,500–9,300, apt 6,170–7,800). The entire "land trades above built-up" claim depends on dropping **G Square Avante at 5,990** — a branded-developer plot dumped on a hunch ("almost certainly peripheral"). Retain it and n=5 gives ~6,000–9,300 and no inversion. An 800-rupee-wide band from n=4 is false precision regardless.

### TIER 4 — Wide bands on almost no evidence

No band trips the 4×-min test — but that's the wrong test. The real problem is **2×+ spread on ≤3 points**:

- **Villyampakkam** 600–1,500 (2.5×, **n=2**) — and there is a **second record, "Villiambakkam," in the same dataset returning `found:false`**. Same village, two records, one asserting a band and one saying no data exists. Resolve or delete.
- **Mugaiyur** 600–1,300 (2.2×, n=2) — geocoded 12.345/79.999, ~90 km out, **outside the CMA entirely**. Don't band it; drop the record.
- **Padi** 6,000–12,500 (2.1×) on one in-locality point.
- **Thenpattinam** 1,050–2,500 (2.4×, 2 comps) — honestly flagged as two products, still one field.
- **Urapakkam** 3,250–4,750 — bimodal with **nothing trading between 2,900 and 4,400**. The band's midpoint describes no property that exists.
- **Uthiramerur** 1,430–1,500 — **n=1**. A 70-rupee band from a single layout's internal range is the most misleadingly precise number in the dataset. Meanwhile **Sirupinayur** — a village *inside Uthiramerur taluk* — gets 1,150–1,550 from nine Uthiramerur listings. Adopt 1,150–1,550 for both.
- **Sriperumbudur apt 3,400–5,950** — only 2 of 5 projects are in-locality, and they are 2,750 and 6,004. That "IQR" is 60% Oragadam. Null it or relabel corridor-blend.
- **Thiruverkadu** — plot and apt bands are 2-point brackets each, overlapping heavily (4,600–6,700 vs 5,300–6,400). Neither is measured.

### Data-hygiene items worth one line each

- **Kattankulathur `rental_yield_pct: 12.9`** — from a developer marketing blog, 3× the city norm. It is caveated in a sibling field no consumer will read. **Null it.**
- `rental_yield_pct` silently mixes sourced figures with admitted guesses (Poonamallee 3.5, Anna Nagar 3.0, Nungambakkam 2.8 are all invented in the notes). The field looks measured. It isn't.
- `trend` likewise mixes real YoY series with "carried from the prior record, not re-verified" (Perumbakkam, Manapakkam, Kanchipuram town) and "inferred from the listing field, not a sourced index" (Tambaram, Sholinganallur, Ambattur, Porur, Thirumudivakkam).
- **Stated n ≠ array length** in at least seven records: Pudupakkam (20 claimed / 14 present), Sriperumbudur (21/12), Thiruvottiyur (18/14), Sithalapakkam (11/9), Walajabad (15/13), Kolathur (6/3), Perumbakkam (8/7). The sample size cannot be audited from the record.
- **Geocode collisions from registry centroids**: Sirupinayur and Kolivakkam both sit at 12.834/79.704, which is Kanchipuram town's own coordinate. Mugaiyur's 12.345/79.999 is also Vanavil Bala Beach's coordinate in the Thenpattinam record. These are fallbacks, not locations.
- **Retire**: `West Chennai` (a zone, not a locality — and it is parking two *curated* projects that should be driving persona lenses), `Ninnaijarai` (unresolvable, almost certainly a mangled TNRERA village string).
- **Suspicious shared endpoints** worth a second look, likely anchoring rather than coincidence: Thoraipakkam plot-max = apt-max = **12,000**; Sithalapakkam plot-max = apt-max = **6,100**; Thiruvottiyur plot-min = apt-min = **6,500**.
