Deals & distressed assets

Bank e-auction properties (SARFAESI), stalled projects, and insolvency opportunities across the Chennai Metropolitan Area, with reserve prices compared to our locality price bands.

The client-lens switcher below buckets deals by ticket size and opportunity type: Boardroom for ₹1Cr+ auctions and institutional-scale plays, Executive for ₹30L–1Cr listings, Value & Distressed for the deepest discounts and retail-accessible resales.

⚠ Legal due diligence is mandatory. Auction and distressed purchases carry title, litigation, occupancy, and dues risks that this tracker cannot verify. "Symbolic possession" means the occupants may still be in the property. Always run an independent lawyer's title search, inspect the original bank notice, and verify dues before bidding. Discount figures compare the reserve price to our locality band midpoint — they are indicative, not appraisals.
